Questions

What gyms ask us

Do my members need to download anything?

Only if they want the member app. The desk can always check anyone in without it.

Can a member check in from home with a photo of the poster?

No. The app sends the phone's location and the gym's fence refuses anything outside it. You can also turn scanning off and use location alone.

What about face recognition or a turnstile?

We do not do it. A desk, a poster and a phone are enough for a gym, and they cost nothing to run.

Is my data mine?

Yes. Export any list as a spreadsheet any time. If you leave, we hand you everything and delete our copy.

Can I try it first?

A demo gym with three months of real-looking data is one call away. Then a 14-day trial on your own gym.

What do I need?

A laptop with a browser for the owner, and phones with the app for staff and members. No server, no installation, no card reader.

Run the gym, not the register.

Twenty minutes on a call, or at your gym if you are in Vadodara. Bring your member register; we will show you your gym inside it.
